## What Industry Leaders Consistently Do Well

1. Unify multiple signal types in one operational view.
2. Treat readiness as both subjective and objective.
3. Make compliance easy with mobile capture, reminders, and kiosk modes.
4. Support both individual and team-level trend interpretation.
5. Tie monitoring to action, not only reporting.
6. Preserve secure role-based access across staff groups.
7. Keep exports, imports, and integrations first-class.
8. Support medical, recovery, and participation workflows alongside training.

## Best-Fit Feature Themes For Habigoal

These are the highest-value patterns for Habigoal's domain:

1. Daily readiness collection that is coach-friendly and athlete-lightweight.
2. High-signal dashboards for coaches, not generic BI.
3. Participation and modification guidance, not only scores.
4. Youth-specific health, maturation, and family-facing trust tools.
5. Structured support actions and documented follow-up loops.

## Translation Into Product Direction

Habigoal should not try to become a full pro-club AMS in one step. The best product path is:

1. Own the daily coach decision loop.
2. Add the minimum integrations that increase signal quality.
3. Expand into health, participation, and planning workflows that make readiness actionable.
4. Preserve a youth-athlete-first experience with privacy and parent trust built in.
